What Cycles is

Not another workout logger.
A coach that understands you.

Most "AI" fitness apps end at the spreadsheet. Cycles starts where a real coach does — with your goals, your constraints, and the messy reality of staying consistent.

01

Programming grounded in science

Periodization, progressive overload, and recovery modelling — built on the same principles elite coaches use, adapted to where you actually are today. No random workouts. No stalled weights.

02

An AI that listens

It learns what you love, what you hate, what hurts, and what life just threw at you. Preferences stick. Injuries are respected. The plan flexes when you do — and holds firm when it matters.

03

Support that feels human

Empathetic check-ins. Habit nudges backed by behavioural science. Honest feedback when you've earned it. Cycles is the voice that turns occasional gym-goers into people who love the process.

The difference
A logger records what you did. A coach understands why it matters.

Cycles doesn't just write your program and wait. It asks how your sleep was. It notices when three hard weeks have piled up. It knows the difference between a bad day and a bad pattern.

The result is an experience closer to training with a coach who genuinely knows you — available any time, priced for everyone, and quietly pulling you toward the version of yourself you've been trying to become.

How it works

Four steps.
One coach, built around you.

i.

Tell it about you

Goals, schedule, gym setup, injuries, experience. A five-minute conversation — not a twenty-question form.

ii.

Get your first cycle

A structured 4–6 week block built around how you actually live. Not a generic template.

iii.

Train, talk, adjust

Log sets in seconds. Check in with your coach any time. Cycles learns and recalibrates in real time.

iv.

Progress, sustainably

Each cycle builds on the last. You outgrow plateaus, not the app itself.
